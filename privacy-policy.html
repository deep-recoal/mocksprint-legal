MockSprint Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11-Oct-2026

This policy explains what information the MockSprint app (the "app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is provided by MockSprint ("we", "us").

Information we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Account details: your email address and/or phone number, and a user ID we assign to youTo create and sign you in to your account, and to keep your progress linked to you.
Sign-in credentials, such as a password or one-time codeTo verify that you are the owner of the account.
Purchase information: which products you bought and the purchase token issued by Google PlayTo confirm your purchase with Google and unlock the content you paid for, including after reinstalling or switching devices.
Your activity in the app, such as tests attempted, answers, scores and progressTo show your results and progress, and to provide the app's features.

We do not collect your payment card details. Payments are processed by Google Play, which handles them under its own privacy policy.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or files.

How we use your information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the app and your account.
  • To verify purchases and unlock paid content.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To keep the app secure and prevent fraud or misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with services needed to run the app:

  • Google Play: to process purchases and verify them through the Google Play Developer API.
  • DigitalOcean: which stores our backend data on our behalf.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account information and activity for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it as described on our account deletion page. We may keep purchase records for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it by emailing [tsthck5@gmail.com].
  • You can request account deletion at any time using the link above.

Children

MockSprint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or on the store listing.
