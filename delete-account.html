Delete your MockSprint account

App: MockSprint · Developer: MockSprint · Last updated: [DATE]

You can ask us to delete your MockSprint account and the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to tsthck5@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. If you signed up with a phone number, send it from any email address and include that phone number.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my MockSprint account.
  3. In the message, include the email address or phone number you use to sign in.
  4. We will reply to confirm your request and delete your account within [30] days.

What we delete

  • Your account details (email address, phone number, user ID)
  • Your sign-in credentials
  • Your test attempts, answers, scores and progress

What we may keep

  • Records of purchases (product, date and Google Play order details), kept for up to [X years] to meet accounting and legal obligations. These are no longer linked to your profile once your account is deleted.

After deletion

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Content you bought may no longer be available on the deleted account. Purchases are managed by Google Play, and refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Questions

Email tsthck5@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
